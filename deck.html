02.

What Get The Fit Does

AI-powered fashion discovery marketplace. Venture-backed. NYC & Dubai.

The conversion machine: A customer types "cute summer outfits for London" into our AI search — and we instantly surface the most relevant pieces from our curated designer catalog. No scrolling through thousands of products. No keyword guessing. Just natural language, like talking to a stylist friend. From there: she watches real people wearing it in shoppable UGC → builds a full outfit with your piece as the anchor → reads your brand story → checks out. No decision fatigue. No leaving to research on TikTok.

AI Chat Discovery

Customers describe what they want in plain language — "Hailey Bieber style dress for a beach wedding" or "old money outfit under $300" — and our AI understands vibes, occasions, and aesthetics. Not just keywords. Real understanding.

Shop the Look — 3 Layers

Your pieces styled in complete, shoppable outfits. Three layers of styling that drive conversion:

  • Brand's own looks: Your lookbook pairings, styled by you. On-model editorial images.
  • Cross-brand AI looks: GTF pairs your pieces with complementary items from other brands. Multi-brand bundled cart. This is the marketplace moat.
  • Closet basics: Your hero piece styled with wardrobe staples the customer already owns. Answers "how do I wear this?" — the #1 conversion blocker.

Virtual Try-On

AI-powered try-on on every product page. Customers see how your pieces look on them before buying. Reduces returns, increases conversion and purchase confidence.

Brand Pages

Your story, your aesthetic, your collection — not a generic product grid. Every brand gets a dedicated page that showcases who you are, with designer profiles, collection stories, and curated shopping.

Shoppable UGC

Real people wearing your pieces. Video-first. Customers tap to shop the exact look — from community content that feels authentic, not ads.

Our Moat: Vibe-Based Discovery

Here's the truth: your brand is unknown to most customers. They don't search for your name — they search for a vibe. "Old Money aesthetic." "Sexy Elegant for Dubai." "Cool Girl going-out look."

GTF maps every brand and product to the vibes trending on social media. When a customer searches by vibe, YOUR pieces surface — even if they've never heard of you. That's how unknown brands become discovered brands.
