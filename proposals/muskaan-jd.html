Head of Brand Partnerships & Business Development

Internal Role Definition · May 2026
"Own the entire brand pipeline — from first DM to signed contract to live products on GTF. You're building GTF's supply side."

Reports to: Atiqa Rahman, CEO

Direct reports: 3 Regional Partnership Managers (MENA, Europe/UK, Asia-Pacific)

Location: Remote / Dubai HQ

1. Brand Acquisition Strategy

  • Own the brand pipeline — decide which brands GTF goes after, in what order, with what pitch
  • Set quarterly targets: X brands onboarded per region, GMV contribution per brand cohort
  • Build the outreach playbook (email templates, HTML previews, PDF decks, follow-up cadence) that the 3 regional managers execute
  • Decide the tier system: which brands get white-glove onboarding vs standard flow
  • Competitive intelligence — know what Revolve, Farfetch, SSENSE, The Outnet are signing and why brands choose them over us
  • Identify emerging brand markets before competitors (e.g. Colombian resort wear, Korean contemporary, Russian eveningwear)

3. Relationship Management

  • Own top-tier brand relationships personally — the ones that move the needle and become case studies
  • Be the face of GTF at trade shows, fashion weeks, and industry events
  • Build referral network — one happy brand introducing you to three more
  • Handle escalations when regional managers get stuck or deals stall
  • Negotiate commercial terms: commission rates, exclusivity windows, marketing commitments, launch timelines

4. Onboarding Oversight

  • Own the signed → live pipeline: contract signed → CSV/images submitted → data QA'd → AI extraction → products live on platform
  • Ensure product data quality — bad CSVs and mislabeled products poison the AI pipeline (learned the hard way)
  • Coordinate with product/engineering on catalog import, image QA, extraction quality, and brand dashboard setup
  • First 30 days of each brand: monitor performance, flag issues, ensure the brand sees early value
  • Create and maintain the onboarding checklist and brand welcome kit

5. Brand Success & Retention

  • Monthly check-ins with active brands — share performance data from the brand dashboard (views, clicks, conversions, vibe performance)
  • Identify upsell opportunities: more SKUs, exclusive drops, UGC campaigns, Shop the Look features, pop-up collaborations
  • Churn prevention — if a brand isn't performing, diagnose why (wrong products? bad images? wrong vibe positioning?) and fix it before they leave
  • Collect brand feedback that informs product roadmap ("brands keep asking for X")
  • Build case studies from top-performing brands to use in future outreach

6. Revenue Accountability

  • Own brand-side GMV targets — you're accountable for the supply side of revenue
  • Track and report: brands onboarded, products live, GMV per brand, commission revenue, pipeline velocity
  • Build the case for which regions/brand-types perform best → allocate team resources accordingly
  • Report to CEO on weekly pipeline health and monthly revenue contribution

What This Role Is NOT

Not marketing — customer acquisition is a separate function. This is brand/supply acquisition.

Not product management — you influence the roadmap with brand feedback, but don't own feature decisions.

Not operations/logistics — shipping, customs, DDP are separate. You flag issues but don't run the ops.

Not content creation — UGC, social media, and editorial are marketing. You provide brands, they provide content.
