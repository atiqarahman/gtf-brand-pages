The Problem

The Discovery Gap Your Customers Face

A woman in New York is looking for something special — a statement jacket that's artisanal, not mass-produced. She wants texture, craftsmanship, something with a story. She searches online. She finds Zimmermann, Ulla Johnson, Dries Van Noten. She can't find you. That sale — and the hand-woven Khaadi jacket she would have fallen in love with — is lost to a brand with a bigger marketing budget.

A Humming Way is something extraordinary: a luxury experimental label that reimagines centuries-old Indian textiles — Khaadi, handloom, vegan fabrics — through a radically contemporary lens. Founded by Sweta Agrawal in 2017, AHW has already showcased at New York Fashion Week twice and Alta Roma in Italy. Every garment is produced at your atelier in Juhu, Mumbai, with fabrics hand-woven by generational artisans in Ahmedabad and Rajasthan.

International runways have validated your design. Now you need a platform that converts that global recognition into global sales — one that can communicate why a hand-woven Khaadi carpet jacket is worth ₹41,000 to a customer who's never touched the fabric.
